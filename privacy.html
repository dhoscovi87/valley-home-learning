Privacy policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This is a plain-language summary of what happens to your information when you visit Valley Home Learning.

No accounts or forms. There are no sign-ups, logins, or forms on this site. We don't ask for your name, email, or anything else.

Visitor counts. We count visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't collect personal data; it only gives us totals like which pages are read and roughly where visitors come from.

Affiliate links. Some links on this site may be affiliate links. If you sign up through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools we think are useful. When you click an affiliate link, that partner's website may set its own tracking cookies so it knows the visit came from us. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

Ads. We may add ads in the future. If we do, ad providers may use cookies, and we'll update this page before that happens.

Children's privacy. This site is written for parents and other adults, not for children. We don't intend to collect any information from children under 13, and nothing on the site asks for it. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Email. If you email us, we only use your message to reply.

Changes. If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
